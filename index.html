About

Most businesses don't need more software. They need the software they already pay for to talk to itself.

I'm Samuele. I started out as a pharmacist, then spent thirteen years on the other side: building audiences, writing the emails that sell, and — increasingly — writing the automations that do the work behind them.

Today I connect the tools a company already uses through their APIs, and I design AI agent pipelines with verification gates and a human in the loop, so nothing goes out unchecked. The result is usually unglamorous and very valuable: an order that files itself, a report that arrives without anyone building it, a shift schedule that sorts itself out on Tuesday morning.

I work in English and Italian, from Padova, with clients across Europe and the US.
